## Ohio Tax Modernization Proposal – Fast Enterprises

`👥 State Evaluators` `🎯 Won $50M+ contract` `📅 2022`

**Led development of a winning $50M+ proposal to modernize Ohio's personal income tax system, demonstrating technical expertise and compliance across a complex RFP.**



### Impact

Fast Enterprises was awarded the contract to modernize Ohio's personal income tax system, with an initial value of over $50 million. The proposal successfully demonstrated FAST's technical expertise, business acumen, compliance with complex state requirements, and ability to modernize large-scale public systems. The project was announced in June 2022.

<p><strong>Context:</strong></p>

<p>I led end-to-end development of Fast Enterprises' response to the State of Ohio Department of Taxation's RFP for a new personal income tax system. This formal, highly technical proposal was read by both technical evaluators and non-technical decision makers, requiring clear, persuasive, and unified messaging that demonstrated Fast's expertise and compliance with complex state requirements.</p>

<p><strong>Activities:</strong></p>

<ul>
  <li>Collaborated with technical and functional subject matter experts, project managers, designers, and legal counsel to interpret RFP requirements and align responses</li>
  <li>Managed proposal timelines, status calls, assignments, and review cycles across multiple teams</li>
  <li>Crafted strategic messaging that balanced technical accuracy with persuasive storytelling</li>
  <li>Conducted holistic review of the complete bid; edited for clarity, tone, and responsiveness to RFP requirements</li>
  <li>Applied compliance mapping to ensure all mandatory requirements were addressed</li>
  <li>Delivered a compliant, compelling, and winning proposal on schedule</li>
</ul>
