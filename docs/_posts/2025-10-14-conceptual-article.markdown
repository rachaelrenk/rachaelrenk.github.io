## Securing GitHub Enterprise - GitHub
I authored two companion conceptual topics that explain GitHub Enterprise’s two distinct approaches to identity and access management, managed users and SSO for personal accounts. Together, these guides help administrators understand the two types of enterprises, and what each model means for user authentication and access control.

#### Context
These topics were developed as part of the [GitHub Enterprise onboarding initiative](enterprise-onboarding-guide.html) to support customers at a critical decision point in the setup process: choosing their enterprise type. Each type, managed users or personal accounts, determines how authentication, provisioning, and access are managed. The documentation needed to clearly distinguish between the two models, helping customers make an informed choice and quickly grasp the implications and requirements of their selected path.

**Role:** Technical Writer

**Date:** 2025

**Activities:**

* Partnered with product managers to clarify and document two parallel identity and access models
* Developed content structures that reflect branching user journeys and clearly direct readers to the appropriate article for their enterprise type
* Authored both topics to explain complex security architecture in accessible, actionable language
* Validated accuracy and clarity through iterative reviews with security SMEs and product teams
